# PROMPTS: Meta Ads Autopilot by Ratnakar Content

Copy-paste these into Claude Code. Write in English, Hinglish or any language you're comfortable with; Claude picks the right script by itself.
Anything that changes your ads waits for your "yes" first.

## 0. Setup check
- `Check my ads setup`
- `Are Meta, Shopify and Shiprocket all connected?`

## 1. Campaign in one line (always created PAUSED)
- `Create a ₹500 sales campaign for tomorrow`
- `Create a ₹500 sales campaign for tomorrow with this video: video ID <id>, link https://<your-store>/products/<handle>, text "<primary text>", headline "<headline>"`
- `Take my old winning ad (creative ID <id>) and make a new ₹700/day campaign starting 1 Nov`
- After the summary: `Yes, create it PAUSED` → after it's created: `Yes, turn it ON now`

## 2. Daily report
- `Send me today's report`
- `Which ads are working and which ones are burning cash?`
- `Give me yesterday's report` / `Give me the last 3 days`

## 3. Real profit
- `Am I really in profit?`
- `What was my real profit today after product cost, shipping, COD and RTO?`
- `Show my real profit for the last 7 days and the gap between Meta and Shopify orders`

## 4. Profit by product
- `Profit by product?`
- `Which product is earning and which one is quietly losing money? Include break-even ROAS`
- `Give me a product-wise P&L for the last 14 days`

## 5. Fix my ads (pause losers, scale winners)
- `Fix my ads`
- `Give me a plan to pause the ads burning cash with no sales, and to scale the winners`
- After the plan: `Only do 1 and 3` / `Yes, do all of them` / `Don't change anything`
- `This campaign was restarted on 18 Oct, decide using only the data since then`

## 6. Comment guard
- `Clean my ad comments`
- `Show me spam and abusive comments on my ads. Keep complaints separate and don't hide them`
- After the list: `Hide 1, 2 and 5` / `Write a reply for the complaint`

## 7. Orders + RTO
- `Orders + RTO today?`
- `How many orders today, how many COD and how many prepaid? What's my RTO rate?`
- `Which shipments are in NDR?`

## Combined
- `Good morning! Give me yesterday's report, real profit and orders + RTO together`
- `This week's summary: spend, clean orders, profit, best and worst product`

Tip: when you mention dates, Claude turns them into `--date`, `--days` or `--since/--until` ("yesterday", "last 7 days", "1 to 15 Oct").
Hinglish works too, e.g. `Kal ke liye 500 ka sales campaign bana do` or `Aaj ki report bhejo`.
